## Data Limitations

Google Play displays cumulative download thresholds,
not exact download totals.

These figures do not establish active users,
paying customers, revenue or market share.

Holly Health is an adjacent competitor because
it focuses on broader health and habit coaching.

Data collected: 21 September 2026.
