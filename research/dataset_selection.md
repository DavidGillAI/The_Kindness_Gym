# Round 1 Dataset Selection

## Purpose

The Round 1 analysis uses small datasets assembled from public reports and app-store listings, alongside an exploratory primary survey.

The datasets were selected to examine three questions:

1. Is there activity in the wider digital wellness market?
2. Have comparable products achieved measurable adoption?
3. What commercial patterns and user concerns should a pilot test?

## Public Market Data

### Sensor Tower: Health & Fitness App Downloads

Source: Sensor Tower, State of Mobile 2026.

The Tableau workbook contains annual worldwide Health & Fitness app download totals prepared from published quarterly figures for 2022–2025.

This dataset provides broad market context. It does not measure demand for kindness training or predict adoption of The Kindness Gym.

### RevenueCat: Subscription App Benchmarks

Source: RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026.

The selected data covers:

- Day-35 download-to-paid conversion for freemium and hard-paywall apps
- Year 1 revenue per paying customer for AI and non-AI subscription apps
- Twelve-month subscriber retention for AI and non-AI apps

These benchmarks were selected to support discussion of monetisation and retention risks. They cover many app categories and should not be treated as forecasts for The Kindness Gym.

### Google Play: Comparable App Milestones

Source: Public Google Play Store listings collected on 21 September 2026.

The dataset records published cumulative download thresholds for:

- Loving Kindness
- The Self-Compassion App
- BeKind
- Holly Health

These figures show minimum download milestones. They do not reveal active users, paying customers, revenue, retention or market share.

## Primary Survey Data

The project also uses an anonymous convenience sample of 90 respondents recruited through an extended personal and social network.

The Round 1 dataset was frozen at 90 responses so that the analysis and charts remained internally consistent.

This survey measures self-reported difficulties, feature preferences, stated interest and concerns. It does not demonstrate representative demand, purchasing behaviour or product effectiveness.

## Selection Rationale

Together, these datasets support a preliminary stakeholder discussion covering market context, comparable products, commercial assumptions and exploratory user feedback.

They are suitable for Round 1 discovery and pitch preparation. A pilot would still be required to measure real engagement, retention, willingness to pay and behaviour change.
