# The Kindness Gym: Round 1 Use Cases

## Use Case 1: Everyday Kindness Coaching

A user describes a real-life situation involving themselves or another person.

The AI responds with:
- a brief acknowledgement;
- a balanced perspective;
- one practical kindness exercise;
- one optional reflection question;
- a relevant research insight only when suitable.

**Example situations:**
- Feeling guilty about saying no to a friend.
- Regretting a mistake at work.
- Feeling unsure about whether to help someone financially.

**Value:**
Provides immediate, personalised support for practising kindness while respecting boundaries and accountability.

---

## Use Case 2: Structured 21-Day Kindness Course

Users follow a 21-day programme designed to strengthen two areas:
- kindness towards themselves;
- kindness towards others.

Each day includes a short exercise or reflection.

AI personalisation could adapt exercises to the user's current situation while keeping the programme structured.

**Value:**
Turns isolated kindness advice into a repeatable habit-building experience.

---

## Use Case 3: Evidence-Based Kindness Insights

Where relevant, the app provides short research-based insights connected to the user's situation.

Research is drawn only from a curated, verified source library.

If no directly relevant evidence exists, the research section is omitted.

**Value:**
Adds educational context and differentiation from generic motivational or wellbeing apps.

**Current limitation:**
POC testing shows that research relevance and citation formatting are not yet reliable enough for production use.

## Fit for the Proposed Company Size

These three use cases suit a small early-stage company because they can share one focused AI guidance system and be tested without building a large product ecosystem.

Everyday Kindness Coaching provides the core capability demonstrated by the current n8n POC. The 21-day course adds structure using curated content, while evidence-based insights can begin with a small manually verified research library.

The pilot should concentrate on coaching and practical exercises first. Survey respondents showed greater interest in progress tracking, self-kindness exercises and personalised exercises than in the proposed 21-day course. Research retrieval and citation handling also require technical improvement before the evidence feature can be trusted in a public product.
