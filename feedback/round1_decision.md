# Round 1 Decision

## Feedback summary

- Colleagues found the purpose clear and liked the early survey, the human-centred idea and the attempt to distinguish the app from therapy.
- They felt the first use case was too broad. They also asked who would pay, what measurable benefit the app provides, and why someone would use it instead of a general LLM.
- Their main safety concern was that users might mistake AI responses for psychological or professional advice. They recommended clearer boundaries and more consistent testing.
- They suggested a better user journey, more personalisation and ways to track progress or acts of kindness. One person proposed a workplace reflection assistant as an alternative direction.
- The reported average peer rating was 3.67.
- Teaching staff feedback: Isabella liked the project overall, especially that I collected real survey data and adapted the project around it while staying true to its purpose. She asked to discuss my next steps so we can shape the project further. She has not yet recommended a specific change of use case.

## Decision

KEEP: The Kindness Gym, in the personal-development sector, with everyday kindness coaching as the core use case.

## Why

The working n8n POC demonstrates the core interaction, and the survey gives us specific situations to investigate. The five-case evaluation also exposed clear problems with research relevance, citations and unsupported reassurance. These are reasons to improve and test the use case further, rather than change direction. The survey does not yet establish market demand.

## Round 2 focus

- Build one working end-to-end coaching capability as the MVP.
- Test it with a LangSmith dataset and experiment, including the cases that failed in Round 1.
- Make research selection and citation formatting more reliable.
- Complete the privacy, compliance, ROI and pilot plans before wider use.
