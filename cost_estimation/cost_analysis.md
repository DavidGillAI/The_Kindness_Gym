# The Kindness Gym: Round 1 Cost Estimate

## Purpose

This is a rough early-stage estimate of what would be required to move from the current proof of concept towards a small pilot.

The figures are preliminary and intended for an investor conversation, not as a final budget.

## Key Assumptions

- The current POC already exists in n8n.
- The first pilot would focus on one core experience: the 21-day kindness course plus personalised AI guidance.
- Development would remain lightweight and use existing cloud tools where practical.
- No large internal engineering team is assumed.
- Research content would be curated manually at first rather than using a vector database.
- Marketing spend would be limited during the pilot phase.
- The founder will complete much of the product development using AI-assisted coding and workflow tools.
- AI development support is treated as a productivity advantage, not as zero-cost labour.
- A limited contingency budget is included for specialist freelance support where required, such as UI/UX, mobile packaging, security review or deployment.

## Preliminary Pilot Budget

| Cost area | Low estimate | High estimate |
|---|---:|---:|
| AI API, workflow automation and hosting | €100 | €300 |
| Specialist design or development support | €800 | €2,000 |
| Privacy and security review | €300 | €800 |
| Pilot participant recruitment and user testing | €100 | €300 |
| Basic launch and presentation assets | €100 | €300 |
| Contingency | €300 | €800 |
| **Estimated total** | **€1,700** | **€4,500** |

## Estimate Limitations

This estimate excludes the founder's unpaid time and assumes a tightly scoped six-week pilot using existing cloud and automation tools.

The final cost will depend on the amount of specialist support required, provider pricing, the selected technical architecture and the privacy safeguards identified before testing with real users.

These figures support an early funding conversation. They are not supplier quotations or a final implementation budget.

## Notes

This estimate assumes the founder completes most product development using AI-assisted tools.

The largest variable is specialist support. Costs could be lower if the founder completes design, deployment and testing independently, or higher if a native mobile app and professional security review are required.
