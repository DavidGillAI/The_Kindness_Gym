# The Kindness Gym: Round 1 Timeline Estimate

## Pilot Goal

Move from the current n8n proof of concept to a small, testable pilot focused on the 21-day kindness course and personalised AI guidance.

## Estimated Timeline

### Weeks 1–2: Product Definition and Design
- Finalise the 21-day course structure
- Define core user journeys
- Refine safety and boundary rules
- Review survey findings and update assumptions

### Weeks 3–4: Pilot Build
- Build the core user experience
- Connect AI guidance to the 21-day programme
- Separate research retrieval from response generation
- Add basic data handling and privacy controls

### Week 5: Testing and Refinement
- Run repeatable evaluation cases
- Test citation reliability and research relevance
- Fix major usability and safety issues

### Week 6: Small Pilot Launch
- Recruit a limited pilot group
- Track completion, repeat use and user feedback
- Measure perceived usefulness and willingness to pay

## Total Estimated Time

**Approximately 6 weeks to a small pilot**, assuming a founder-led, AI-assisted build with limited specialist support.

## Key Dependency

The timeline depends on keeping the pilot tightly scoped. Native mobile development, advanced analytics, vector search or complex social features would extend the schedule.
