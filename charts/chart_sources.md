# Chart Sources

## Chart 1: Health & Fitness App Downloads, 2022–2025

### Source
Sensor Tower, State of Mobile 2026.

Supporting data files:
- health_fitness_downloads_2022_2025_worldwide.csv
- health_fitness_downloads_annual_2022_2025_worldwide.csv

### Metric
Worldwide Health & Fitness app downloads, aggregated from quarterly data into annual totals for 2022–2025.

### Limitation
This is a broad Health & Fitness app category and does not measure kindness, self-compassion or personal-development apps specifically.

Download volume also does not establish active users, retention, paying customers or demand for The Kindness Gym.

### Interpretation
The chart demonstrates sustained activity in the broader Health & Fitness mobile-app market.

It should be used as market context rather than evidence of demand for The Kindness Gym.


## Chart 2: Download-to-Paid Conversion Within 35 Days

### Source
RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026.

Supporting data file:
- Kindness Gym Market Data - Subscription Conversion.csv

### Metric
Median percentage of app downloads that resulted in at least one paid subscription within 35 days.

Freemium: 2.1%
Hard paywall: 10.7%

### Limitation
These are aggregated subscription-app benchmarks from apps using RevenueCat and are not specific to kindness or personal-development products.

The figures do not establish that using a hard paywall would produce the same result for The Kindness Gym.

### Interpretation
The chart illustrates how monetisation strategy can be associated with large differences in early conversion.

It informs business-model discussion but does not determine which model The Kindness Gym should use.


## Chart 3: Year 1 Revenue per Paying Customer: AI vs Non-AI

### Source
RevenueCat, State of Subscription Apps 2026.

Supporting data file:
- Kindness Gym Market Data - AI Revenue and Retention.csv

### Metric
Year 1 realised lifetime value per paying customer for AI-powered and non-AI subscription apps.

### Limitation
These are aggregated benchmarks across many app categories.

AI classification refers to apps where AI or machine learning provides the primary value. The data does not predict the revenue that The Kindness Gym would generate.

Revenue performance should also be considered alongside retention, as RevenueCat reports that AI apps can monetise more strongly while experiencing higher churn.

### Interpretation
The chart provides evidence that AI-powered subscription apps can achieve higher revenue per payer.

It should be treated as a benchmark for commercial context, not as a forecast for The Kindness Gym.

## Chart 4: Comparable Wellness Apps: Google Play Download Milestones

### Source
Google Play Store listings collected 21 September 2026.

Apps included:
- Loving Kindness
- The Self-Compassion App
- BeKind: Acts of Kindness Ideas
- Holly Health

### Metric
Publicly displayed Google Play cumulative download thresholds.

### Limitation
These figures are minimum download milestones rather than exact download totals. They do not establish active users, paying customers, revenue, retention or market share.

Holly Health is an adjacent wellbeing product rather than a direct kindness-training competitor.

### Interpretation
The chart shows that comparable kindness, compassion and wellbeing apps have achieved measurable consumer adoption on Google Play.

It should not be presented as proof of demand for The Kindness Gym itself.
